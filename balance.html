Balance Analysis

Balance Status All invariants satisfied

Shield mechanic: binary per hit — a single hit either bounces off fully (hit ≤ shield, shield value UNCHANGED) or shatters the shield with overflow to HP (hit > shield). Burst spells fire independent projectiles; each is compared separately. The shield absorbs exactly shield_strength worth of damage when it shatters, so a higher-rarity spell still sees meaningful mitigation from a lower-rarity shield.

Core Invariant — N+1 Offense vs N Defense

Same-tier: equal chain → defense wins; one extra tile on offense → offense wins.
Higher-rarity offense: offense wins at any equal chain length, but shield absorbs shield_strength damage (overflow to HP). Ceiling rule: defense_base < offense_base × 12/11. Checked at all chain lengths 1–8.
