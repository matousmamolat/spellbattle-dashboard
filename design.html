Design Reference

Load-bearing design rules

  • Heroes define identity, not elements (same element can feel distinct across heroes)
  • No AoE
  • Spell-as-fuel: same-element spells in a chain count as element tiles at EQUAL weight (no bonus)
  • Binary shields, per-shield persistence (revised 2026-09-14 — supersedes the 2026-09-07
  • shot" version): a shield always fully blocks any hit ≤ its strength
  • with-overflow on a hit > its strength
  • Persistent shields (`OneHitShield = false` — Shield, Big Shield, Massive Shield,
  • One-hit shields (`OneHitShield = true` — Root Braid, Spider Web ONLY, per the SSH
