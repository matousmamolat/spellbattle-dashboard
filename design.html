Design Reference

Load-bearing design rules

  • Heroes define identity, not elements (same element can feel distinct across heroes)
  • No AoE
  • Spell-as-fuel: same-element spells in a chain count as element tiles at EQUAL weight (no bonus)
  • Binary shields: full block, OR break with overflow damage passing through
  • Support spells can be canceled mid-cast by overpowering damage
  • Match level cap of 4; Basic / Rare / Epic / Legendary unlock at levels 1–4
